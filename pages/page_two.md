## The disadvantages of open source software

While open source software has many advantages, some of which are mentioned on page 1, there are also certain risks and downsides attached to the choice of using open source rather than keeping the software closed.

The most pressing risks and issues of open source software as identified by Haider et. al from 2023 are bugs in source code, insufficient product documentation, lack of appropriate communication and coordination among developers, lack of knowledge and lack of project management practices (63337). These issues are classified as critical. All of the aforementioned issues could lead to being severe security risks of they are allowed to get out of hand. Particularly bugs, when left unpatched or going undetected, have the ability to destroy the entire software. Some other potential issues proposed are ones such as *unanticipated code contamination* or *license mistreat and violation* (Haider et al. 2023, 63338), which could cause the software supplier to face legal repercussions despite technically not being the ones who broke the law. 

A major security risk of open source software, while generally being more of an advantage, is that *everyone* has access to it. While this allows more people to fix issues and expand the software’s capabilities, it also allows someone with nefarious intent to deliberately cause harm. 

Overall, while open source software is considered a beneficial option, it is not without risks, which are mainly related to the software’s security.
